# Evidence vocabulary

Use original requirement and scenario IDs. A source-read finding is not an executed failure. A component, sampled model, build or bridge pass proves only its recorded scope. Native input, physical presentation, AT/IME, resources and deployment need their applicable original observations. Independent accepted scenarios and whole requirements are different from release acceptance.

Record immutable source revision and evidence/reviewer hashes for acceptance references. Preserve historical partial and failed reports on their original tuples; use explicit relevance when reusing them after a change. A dispatch-time surface gate is not automatically a payload or target-view identity. Successful teardown does not imply normal host exit or successful behavior.

Architecture ideas are proposed, adopted, rejected or deferred with reasons; studies and diagrams are not delivered refactors. Graphify is navigation: CPU AST and lexical/inferred edges have limits, and CUDA embeddings do not prove correctness. Current Elm 0.19 guidance must be distinguished from historical Signal-era FRP research.
