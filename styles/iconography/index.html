Identity and iconography

A folded W and a central red diamond anchor the Warlock identity.

Folded W

The mark retains two valleys and a central diamond. Neutral facets support one red focal diamond. Keep clear space at least one diamond width around the mark.

Size and clear space

Use the detailed mark at 32px and above, monochrome at 24–31px, and a plain W below 24px. Essential small marks use approved foreground colors.

Utility icons

Utility icons use a 20px viewbox and 1.5px strokes, with 16px compact and 24px prominent variants. Shape and an accessible name identify an action; color alone does not.

Accessible names

Hide decorative icons from accessibility APIs. Give icon-only controls a meaningful accessible name that describes the action.
