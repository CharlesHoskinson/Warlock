Motion

Immediate, stable interactions keep focus and feedback aligned with current facts.

Immediate shell interaction

Shell surfaces open and close at 0ms. Focus, selection, disabled state, checkmarks, outcome text and geometry change immediately. Theme changes are immediate.

Pointer-fill timings

Press-in is 60ms, state and release 90ms, soft enter 120ms and soft exit 60ms. Only enabled pointer-fill feedback transitions. No control moves, scales or waits for a decorative effect.

Observed-window gating

A portal begins only after a new application window is observed mapped; closing follows observed removal of the same incarnation. Launch intent, receipts, focus changes, restoration, snap and workspace navigation do not trigger a portal.

Reduced motion

Reduced motion makes durations zero and removes the portal while preserving static depth and sharp cues. High contrast and forced colors turn effects off.

Interruption and completion

Hidden documents, preference changes, stale incarnations and interruption settle immediately. Completion removes decoration only; it never claims operation success or focus acknowledgement. Explore the portal.
