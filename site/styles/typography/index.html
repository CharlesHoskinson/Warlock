Typography

Local type families bring a precise hierarchy to desktop controls and reading pages.

Families

Space Grotesk Bold sets headings of at least 20px. Inter sets body and interface text. JetBrains Mono Regular sets code, paths and shortcuts. Fonts use their supplied weights, local files and font-display: swap.

Reading and scaling

Reading measure is at most 70ch. Labels and titles wrap without shrinking; taskbar application names may ellipsize while retaining their full accessible names. Text scale applies once at the root.

Paths, numbers and shortcuts

Use tabular lining numerals for numeric tables, counts and percentages. Code type distinguishes paths and shortcuts; ordinary feedback stays in Inter.
