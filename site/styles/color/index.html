Approved contrast pairs

Night text on surface is 15.89:1; Dawn text on surface is 18.10:1. Night on-action on default action fill is 6.49:1; Dawn is 7.62:1. High contrast black on white is 21:1. Download the approved contrast manifest.

Brand and danger

Red denotes identity, active application contours, selection bars and affirmative action. Danger uses a neutral surface, danger outline, stop symbol, reason and explicit action label.

Disabled and selected

Disabled text uses text-secondary at full opacity with disabled semantics and an unavailable cue. Selection combines selected fill with a leading bar and a check or existing selected wording.

These labeled samples hold each state still for comparison. The disabled example is unavailable; the danger sample shows neutral destructive chrome.
