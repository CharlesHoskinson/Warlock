Warlock for Omarchy

A Windows-style desktop for Hyprland

Warlock gives Omarchy a taskbar, a Start-style launcher, Alt-Tab, Task View, snap layouts and a notification center. Hyprland keeps its tiling, input and speed; Warlock adds the desktop you already know how to use.

Try the desktopSee what it does

The test drive runs Warlock's real Elm interface on a simulated desktop, right in your browser.

What it does

The desktop you know, on the compositor you love

Taskbar

Your apps, one bar away

Pinned and running applications share the taskbar. A pill marks every running app and turns red for the one you are using. Start, Task View, Alt-Tab, notifications, Files, System and Settings sit beside them.

Try the taskbar

Launcher

Search, then launch

Open Applications, type a name and launch it. The top result carries the application's own actions and recent files, and pinned apps can be reordered right there.

Try the launcher

Alt-Tab

Switch windows the way you expect

Hold Alt and tap Tab to move through open windows. The selected window stands out before you let go, and Escape leaves everything where it was.

Try Alt-Tab

Task View

Every desktop at a glance

See each workspace and its windows, move a window to another desktop, or go straight to the one you need.

Try Task View

Snap layouts

Snap into place

Choose a half or a quarter of the screen from the snap chooser. Warlock asks Hyprland to place the window and shows where it landed.

Try snap layouts

Notifications

Stay in the loop

Live notifications keep their actions, the history keeps what you missed, and do not disturb holds the rest while you focus.

Try notifications

Settings

Make it yours

Night, Dawn or high contrast, text size, motion and visual effects. Review each change, then save it.

Try Settings

Files

Your folders, one click away

Open Home, Recent or a collection from quick-access tiles, or type a folder path. Warlock opens the location in Omarchy's Files application.

Try Files

Built on Elm

Honest about every window

Warlock asks Hyprland to act and shows what Hyprland reports. One Elm interface drives every surface, so the taskbar, the launcher and the switcher always agree.
