Window picker

The picker selects a window family from an application group with more than one family.

Overview

The picker selects a window family from an application group with more than one family.

Anatomy

Family controls pair labels and detail with preview wells where available.

Each family is one actionable control with an Activate or Restore label, detail and a reserved preview area. The preview is inside the control, not a separate button.

States

Live images and application fallback retain the same target geometry. Unavailable controls communicate their state.

  • Open: Activate identifies the family action.
  • Minimized: Restore identifies the action.
  • Preview unavailable: fallback content preserves the family target.
  • Blocked: the control reports why it cannot currently act.

Specimen

Behavior

Choose a family to activate it. A held target remains stable as preview content changes; dismissing the popup returns focus through the controller.

Choose a family rather than its image. The popup returns focus through its opener when dismissed; a new preview frame does not move the held control.

Accessibility

Controls retain names even when a preview cannot paint. Keyboard focus and family selection are distinct.

Content

Identify the family in text; describe the preview state rather than treating an image as the only label.

Keep the full family identity in the control name. A fallback identifies the application and does not claim to be a live window image.
