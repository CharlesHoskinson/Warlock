# Settings

Settings is a single scrollable preference surface laid out like a Windows Settings page: a help expander, switch rows, segmented choices, theme swatch cards, a primary Save for each task and shortcut routes as cards of radio rows. Its title and status precede the keyed control list. Close and Show help lead the list. Expanded help appears directly after its disclosure; deferred appearance toggles, motion, theme and scale, appearance save, then shortcut choices follow in source order. Each shortcut route shows its observed state, Keep, Default and Alternative choices, and the availability details supplied by the host.

## Anatomy and hierarchy

The renderer root is `.surface-popup[data-mode="settings"]`; its children are `h1`, `p[role=status][aria-live=off]`, and `.surface-controls[role=group]`. Rows are `button.control-utility` with `.control-label` and `.control-detail`; help and shortcut observations are `p.notification-text[data-settings-help]` and `p.notification-text[data-shortcut-state]`. Selectors use the actual `data-surface-control` identities. The flat source order is authoritative. There are no tabs, sidebar, rearranged rows or invented headings; every group is drawn from consecutive pieces. The controls form a twelve-column grid with 8px column gaps and no row gap, so grouped pieces meet without seams; a control spans all twelve columns unless its group divides the row. Vertical rhythm comes from margins: the help expander sits 6px below Close; the appearance switch rows, the motion group and the theme cards each start 20px after the preceding control; text sizes follow the theme cards by 8px; a save group follows its choices by 14px and its Refresh follows by 6px; the shortcut notice starts 22px down and each shortcut route card 10px after the previous one. Single-line rows are at least 36px high and rows with a detail at least 56px; with a coarse pointer they are at least 44px and 60px. Each control keeps its own border width and padding in every state; selection changes colour and decoration only. Controls have 10px corners unless stated below. The surface uses the shell skin's 20px inset, a 6px scroll gutter and a 640px documentation width. The native allocation stays authoritative. Long content wraps and grows. On short surfaces the list scrolls; no sticky header hides focus.

The two deferred appearance choices are pressed buttons that the Settings stylesheet draws as switch rows; they keep button semantics and `aria-pressed`. Theme choices are Night theme, Dawn theme and High contrast theme; text size choices are 100%, 125%, 150% and 200%. Motion offers Follow system, Reduced motion and Full motion. Shortcut choices are scoped to Applications, System and Notifications and include existing availability details. Save motion preference, Save settings and Save shortcut choices each own an independent task boundary. The renderer emits each as `.settings-save-group > button + p.settings-save-caption`; the button carries `aria-describedby` to its caption and no `.control-detail`. Each enabled save uses affirmative fill, on-action text and a soft action-coloured glow; effects off and high contrast remove the glow. The caption projection places each explanation in a visible caption outside the fill and associates it with its save button through `aria-describedby`. Refresh and Close remain quiet. Disabled saves remain readable, opaque and unavailable.

## States and behaviour

Each kind of control has its own arrangement and selection cue, always beside the existing wording:

- Switch rows (Disable soft effects, Reduce transparency) are cards: the card fill (text at 5%), a 1px hairline (text at 9%), at least 3.5rem high with 8px 16px 8px 14px padding, 4px apart. The glyph and label sit at the start; the On or Off detail (13px) and a drawn switch, 2.5rem by 1.25rem, sit at the inline end. Off is a track of text at 6% with the essential border and a 0.75rem secondary-text thumb at the start; `aria-pressed=true` fills the track and border with the action colour, moves an on-action thumb to the end (mirrored in right-to-left text) and turns the On wording primary. The row itself never changes with state.
- Motion: the motion status row heads the group with its glyph and a 600 label, its detail aligned with the label text. Follow system, Reduced motion and Full motion follow 6px below as one segmented control, a third of the row each, without glyphs.
- Text size: 100%, 125%, 150% and 200% follow the theme cards as a second segmented control, a quarter of the row each, each previewing its own size with the text-size glyph at 0.875, 1.0625, 1.25 and 1.5rem above the label.
- Segments are 2px apart, with 4px inner corners and 10px outer corners on the ends of the row, at least 3.5rem high, the card fill and hairline, and a centred 13px label. `aria-current=true` gives a segment the brand tint (the action colour at 24%, 10% in Dawn), a brand-text edge at 55%, an 18 × 3px brand pill 5px above its bottom edge and its Selected detail in 11px brand text at 600.
- Theme cards take a third of the row each, with 12px corners, the card fill, a hairline and 6px 6px 10px padding. A 4.25rem swatch with 7px corners leads each card: a small desktop drawn in that theme's own colours, with a bar, two window frames, text lines and an accent. The 18px theme glyph and the 13px name at 600 follow. `aria-current=true` gives the card a brand-text border with a 1px inner brand ring, the brand tint, a 20px check badge in the action colour 12px from its top inline-end corner, and its Selected detail in brand text.
- Shortcut routes: the shortcut notice is a quiet 13px secondary note. Each route is a card: its state paragraph heads it at 600 with 10px upper corners, followed by Keep, Default and Alternative rows on the card fill and the host's chord captions in 12px JetBrains Mono secondary text aligned with the label text; the last piece closes the card with 10px lower corners. Each row shows a 20px radio ring in its glyph slot in secondary text; `aria-current=true` fills the ring with a centred dot in brand text and turns the Selected detail brand text at 600, while the row keeps the card fill.
- A save is at least 2.5rem high and 13rem wide, with a centred 600 label after the save glyph; an enabled save is filled as above (with a brand-text border in Dawn), and a disabled save keeps the card fill and hairline with secondary text. Its caption sits 6px beneath in 12px secondary text.

Focus is the shared blue, 2px offset ring with an opaque gap, independent of selection. Press and hover retain selection: under a hovering fine pointer an unselected card, segment, switch row or radio row takes the stronger card fill (text at 8%), a selected card or segment takes the action colour at 32%, and an enabled save takes the action hover colour. Help is an expander card at least 3.5rem high with the help glyph and a 600 label at the start and the Expanded or Collapsed detail and a drawn 7px chevron at the inline end; the chevron points down when collapsed and up when expanded. Help uses `aria-expanded`; when expanded, the header's lower corners square off and its paragraphs join it as one info card, with 10px padding, text aligned with the label text, 13px titles at 600, 12px text at a 1.45 line height and a rule between paragraphs; the paragraphs grow in normal flow, preserving drafts. Static status is quiet and uses the source wording. Pending, refused and unconfirmed results remain distinct and neutral unless typed outcome hooks support a richer grammar. Refresh reads rather than replaying a mutation; appearance refresh explicitly discards unsaved edits. Shortcut refresh preserves pending choices according to its model.

An appearance draft changes no renderer theme or root scale. `appearance.js` projects the committed `data-theme`, `data-text-scale`, `data-effects` and `data-reduced-transparency` only. Motion preference storage and application are separate facts; a saved preference is not an application acknowledgement. Shortcuts require their own saved and live-binding observations. A receipt alone does not establish application. Effects-off is a deferred appearance choice; the resolved reduced tier is a host capability, not another setting.

Settings enters and exits at 0ms. Draft changes and help expansion do not run a portal. D01 settles incompatible active decorations immediately on committed preference, visibility, capability or motion changes, without relying on an animation event suppressed by `animation:none`. No layout, outline or shadow animates here.

## Keyboard and access

The source opens focus at Close, restores it to the opener on dismissal, and preserves keyed control identity. Tab and Shift+Tab traverse controls; source help describes arrows, Home/End, Enter and Escape. Focus stays visible at scroll edges. The shared focus, high-contrast and forced-colours rules remain authoritative. High contrast paints cards on the opaque surface with the essential border and a text-at-22% tint for selection, and draws a disabled save with a dashed unfocused-edge border. Forced colours make the panel opaque Canvas, draw buttons in ButtonFace and ButtonText with ButtonText borders (Close, Refresh and the motion status stay borderless), unavailable controls in GrayText, the switch track in Canvas with a ButtonText thumb and Highlight with a HighlightText thumb when on, selected cards and segments with a Highlight border, the segment pill, check badge and selected radio in Highlight, and the theme swatches in their own colours with a CanvasText ring. Disabled text keeps opacity 1. Text scales apply once at the root; padding is in CSS px. Use no clipping, ellipsis or text shrinking. Below 26rem of list width, theme cards, text sizes and motion choices each take the full row with 10px corners, so at 320px and 200% zoom content reflows into one column. Reduced transparency replaces the glass panel with the opaque surface.

## Required renderer work

The current flat list has no semantic group headings or group wrappers; the switch rows, segment rows, theme cards and route cards are drawn from adjacency alone, and only the shortcut routes have a source heading. Source-backed, stable group headings with group wrappers and relationships are required for the Appearance, Motion, Theme, Text size and Shortcuts groups to be named; do not manufacture headings with CSS or reorder controls. The switch, radio and check badge are CSS decoration drawn from the existing `aria-pressed` and `aria-current`; the On/Off and Selected wording remains the non-colour cue and the accessible state. Add an unavailable/slashed cue for disabled controls as real decorative renderer markup without changing accessible names. The motion status is currently a disabled button rather than a status paragraph; a source change is needed to express it as noninteractive content. The renderer groups each Save control with a sibling caption in `.settings-save-group`. It preserves each keyed item and control identity, moves the existing detail into the caption and associates it through a stable `aria-describedby` ID. The filled treatment applies only inside that group. Until that integration, the running renderer retains its existing neutral save rows. Existing preview descriptions remain in the accessible description alongside the caption. Typed outcome projection is required for semantic icons; CSS never parses notices. These changes require controller/renderer validation and are not represented as existing markup in the specimen.

## Content-driven geometry

The popup has no fixed height. Its maximum is the host allocation, at most 420px and the available viewport below the taskbar. A flex scrollport holds the keyed list; rows do not shrink and multiline content grows. At short heights the entire popup scrolls. The content-driven documentation panel never reserves an empty 680px. Three independent enabled saves use their actual control identities. An enabled state requires the corresponding source model to be ready and dirty; a receipt does not make another boundary saved.

The host supplies the available popup block size after accounting for the current taskbar geometry. Area CSS fills that allocation and does not subtract a fixed viewport clearance. The list has no row gap; the margins above set the space between groups. Hover applies only to enabled controls on fine hover-capable pointers. A selected unavailable choice keeps its selection cue while retaining disabled text and semantics. A visible unavailable cue requires the renderer enhancement above.

The independent save examples use documentation headings outside the source-shaped renderer. They illustrate distinct task boundaries without introducing headings or wrappers into Elm's flat control list. Each scale fixture has its own document root; the committed scale applies exactly once.
