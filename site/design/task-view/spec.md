# Task View and workspaces

## Overview

Task View presents observed workspaces as Windows-style desktop columns of window cards, with the All windows filter in a toolbar row above them. With one workspace, four or more, or too little width, the same inventory stacks in one column. Filters browse the inventory. A family activates or restores its window. An eligible empty inactive workspace has a separate “Go to workspace N” action. “Move [family]…” enters destination selection. These are separate operations; pressing a filter does not switch desktops.

## Anatomy

`.surface-popup[data-mode="overview"]` contains the Task View heading, a static status paragraph and `.surface-controls`. Controls remain in source order: Close Task View, All windows, each workspace filter followed by its eligible rows, then recovery controls. Each `.overview-workspace` groups its workspace button and only its contiguous family prefix. Move and Go controls can be siblings outside that group. There are no drag gestures or workspace management controls, and no window thumbnails until the renderer supplies them.

The popup is an inline-size container. Desktop columns appear when the controls hold two or three `.overview-workspace` groups and the popup is at least 36rem wide, or exactly two groups at 26rem or wider; the rem thresholds move larger text to the stacked arrangement sooner. The controls then form a grid of two or three equal columns with a 12px column gap. The first row is a toolbar: Close at the start of the first column and the All windows chip at the inline end. Each workspace group takes one column; the group box gives way (`display: contents`) so its header and cards join the column grid while the element keeps its `role="group"` attribute, and every later sibling up to the next group, including Move footers, Go actions and workspace refresh, joins the same column in DOM order. A desktop panel sits behind each column: a 1px hairline, 14px corners, a faint vertical gradient of text from 5.5% to 2.5%, a 1px top highlight, extending 10px below the column's last control. Inside a column, controls sit 8px from the panel edges, the header 8px from its top, and cards 8px apart; titles align across columns because headers and cards keep their content at the top. Refresh closes the view on its own full-width row 20px below the columns.

In the stacked arrangement the controls form one column with 6px gaps, and each workspace group starts 20px after the preceding sibling, including the gap, under a 1px hairline inset 10px.

A workspace header shows the desktop glyph in brand text and the workspace label in Space Grotesk 15px at 600; a non-empty detail, such as Active workspace, sits under the label as a pill of 11px brand text at 600 on a 14% brand tint, aligned with the label text. Headers are at least 2.75rem high. A window card is the raised surface at 92% with a 1px hairline, 10px corners, a 1px shadow and top highlight: one generic 15px window glyph in brand text before the 14px title at 600, the source detail below in 12px secondary text, at least 56px high. Move is a slim footer attached under its card: the card's lower corners square off, and the footer, at least 2.25rem high (2.75rem with a coarse pointer), carries the 10px lower corners, the recessed fill at 72% and a 12px secondary label at 600 after the move glyph. Go is outlined with the essential border at 70%; its detail aligns with its label text. Close and Refresh are quiet controls in secondary text, and Refresh keeps its detail on the same line. The All windows chip has a text-at-16% outline on the tile fill, the grid glyph and a 13px label at 600, with its detail on the same line.

Single-line controls, Move footers, the All windows chip, Refresh and Go are at least 36px high, and window cards at least 56px. With a coarse pointer, single-line controls are at least 44px and family cards and other rows with details at least 60px. Controls have 10px corners. Rows do not shrink and grow for wrapped content. All labels wrap; the viewport and scrollport preserve a 6px focus gutter. Popup geometry belongs to the host; 960px is a documentation preference, not a native allocation.

## States and behaviour

`overview:all` and `overview:workspace:` use `aria-pressed`. A pressed filter has the selected wash (the action fill at 22%, 30% under a hovering pointer and 36% while pressed), a brand-text border at 45%, a 3px brand-text bar on its leading edge and a visible check drawn with decorative geometry: two 2px brand strokes in a leading slot on the All windows chip and in a trailing slot at the top inline end of a workspace header. A pressed workspace filter also tints its desktop panel's hairline with brand text at 35%. The control border width and padding remain constant across selection states. The check and bar retain visibility for a selected unavailable filter in high contrast and forced colours; the unavailable cue paints in its own reserved inline-end slot on the label, so it never replaces the check or the bar. “Active workspace” is independently sourced detail, not selected state. Family detail identifies workspace and Open, Minimized, Unavailable for activation or Awaiting native confirmation. Disabled controls retain opacity and semantics; an unavailable Go keeps a dashed border on the recessed fill. Hover and press never erase selection; a hovered window card shows a stronger hairline; shared focus styling belongs to the actual focused control.

`overview:transfer:` enters an in-place subflow. Source order becomes Cancel transfer followed by eligible `overview:destination:` actions labelled “Move to workspace N”. Cancel transfer sits alone on the first row, and the destinations form a grid of tiles at least 11rem wide with 8px gaps: each tile is at least 5rem high, with 12px 14px padding, 12px corners, a 1px hairline and the tile fill, and shows a 24px desktop glyph in brand text above its 14px label at 600. Cancel returns to the initiating Move control when it remains available, then the workspace filter or All windows. Transfer membership remains at its admitted source while pending or unknown. A receipt alone does not prove movement. `overview:refresh` and `overview:workspace-refresh` read current facts, without retrying a mutation. The status region uses the source wording and `aria-live="off"`.

An observed empty workspace stays visible. Only an eligible inactive empty workspace gets `overview:go:`; its detail says “Switch desktop”. Navigation closes the overview while its result is observed. Filter, navigation and transfer presentation changes are immediate and do not trigger application portals.

## Keyboard and accessibility

Use the renderer's buttons, names, focus identity and source order. Tab reaches each enabled control and Enter or Space activates it. The controller owns opening focus, cancellation and focus return. The shared 2px focus ring, 2px offset and opaque gap remain visible in the scroll gutter. At 320px and enlarged text, labels wrap and rows grow. Forced colours retain a solid selection bar and a border-drawn check, draw cards, chips and actions with ButtonText borders, the desktop panels as Canvas with a CanvasText border and a pressed filter with a Highlight border, and keep colour forcing with a 2px Highlight outline on the focused control. High contrast draws cards, footers, destinations, Go and the chip with the essential border, the desktop panels with the essential border and a pressed filter with a brand-text border; high contrast and effects off remove the card shadows. Shared foundations supply high contrast, focus, motion, coarse target and disabled affordances; this package adds no global recipe.

## Renderer requirements

Keep `data-surface-control` identities `overview:all`, `overview:workspace:`, `overview:family:`, `overview:transfer:`, `overview:destination:`, `overview:go:`, `overview:refresh`, `overview:workspace-refresh`, and `control:close`; retain `aria-pressed`, disabled authority, `data-focus-only`, accessible names and source order. The current `Selected` detail is retained alongside the decorative check. Do not infer active workspace, focus or operation result from text. The desktop columns rest on sibling order: `.overview-workspace` holds only the header and contiguous family prefix, so Move, Go and later controls join a column positionally, and the column layout covers two or three workspaces only. A semantic workspace wrapper that contains every control belonging to its workspace, or a `data-workspace` attribute on each control, is required for structural columns at any workspace count, and a typed active-workspace attribute on the wrapper is required before the active desktop can carry a visual cue beyond its source detail. Window cards need host-provided decorative window thumbnails, keyed to each family and bound to the same publication and lease, with the generic window glyph as the fallback; per-application icons need the same keyed, decorative projection. Neither may change a control's accessible name, identity or order. The renderer currently has no visible transfer context naming the selected family; a source-backed projection is required to add it. No CSS-generated label substitutes for that change.
