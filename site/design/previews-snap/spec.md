# Window previews and snap layouts

## Overview

The picker presents window families as thumbnail cards. A preview describes the family or client image; it does not determine whether the control can activate its window. Snap is a Windows-style snap layouts flyout: six region proposals drawn as two miniature screens, halves and quarters, and a separate Apply action. The popup fills its host allocation and enters and leaves immediately.

## Anatomy

The renderer emits `.surface-popup[data-mode="picker"]`, a heading, a static status and `.surface-controls`. The Close button precedes `button[data-surface-control^="family:"]`. Each family control contains an optional `.preview-content[aria-hidden]`, `.window-preview[data-preview-state]`, image or icon, title, state and fidelity spans, then `.control-label`, `.control-detail` and an optional hidden `.preview-description` referenced by `aria-describedby`. The image is decorative. The action name remains the control's accessible name. `Hidden` has no preview node at all.

Until the host admits a profile, `.surface-popup[data-mode="picker"]` without `data-picker-admitted="true"` arranges the families as a grid of thumbnail cards: auto-fill columns at least 12.5rem wide with 12px gaps, and Close alone on the first row. A card has 8px inline and top and 12px bottom padding, 12px corners, a 1px hairline, the 4% tile fill and a 1px top highlight. Its `.window-preview` is a 16:9 thumbnail well with 8px corners on the recessed surface and an inner hairline; the image fills it with `contain`, and a fallback icon stays 3rem square in its centre. The preview state and fidelity ride on the thumbnail as one pill 6px from its lower inline-start corner: 10px text at 600 on the canvas at 84%, the fidelity in secondary text, with a 6px brand dot before a live state. The 14px label at 600 and the detail follow under the thumbnail. Card height follows its wrapped text, and the 16:9 ratio holds the well's height whether or not an image has arrived; a Hidden family has no preview node, so its card has no well. Before a preview publishes, the card draws an empty well of the same 16:9 size, so the target keeps its geometry when the frame arrives.

Once admitted (`data-picker-admitted="true"`), `preview.css` sets a measured row profile instead. A reserved 160 × 100px well and 12px gap hold each preview. Five measured caption slots retain their positions across image, icon and absent-image states. The normal row's height, minimum and maximum are `min(calc(7.5rem + 16px), calc(100vh - 30px))`. Text wraps. Arrival and release never alter an admitted target's rectangle.

The renderer emits `.surface-popup[data-mode="snap"]`, Close, six `snap:region:` buttons in source order and `snap:apply`. The region buttons are the diagram: left and right halves form one miniature screen, and top left, top right, bottom left and bottom right quarters form a second. Each screen is a frame drawn behind its zones: 14px corners, a 1px hairline, the canvas at 55% and a 1px top highlight, extending 7px beyond the zones. The popup is an inline-size query container. From 34rem of popup width the two screens sit side by side, halves first, separated by a 30px spacer column, so their zones sit 42px apart: each half spans both rows of its screen, and the quarters form a 2 × 2 screen with rows at least 5rem high. Below 34rem the halves screen sits above the quarters screen, separated by a 22px spacer row, so their zones sit 34px apart, and the halves row is at least 5.5rem high. Root text scaling raises that threshold. Zones are muted panes with 8px corners: text at 9% with a text-at-8% hairline and a 1px top highlight, at least 4.75rem high, with a 13px label at 600 and an 11px detail at 600 centred in the zone; the label mirrors the reserved unavailable-cue slot at its inline start, so label, detail and zone share one centre. Close sits alone on the first row as a quiet control. Apply remains a distinct control on the last row at the inline end, 18px below the zones: at least 2.5rem high, 10px corners, the snap glyph and a 14px label at 600. Labels and details retain space and wrap. A snap surface with fewer than six regions places its zones two to a row with 8px gaps.

## States and behaviour

Live and historical drawable images use contain, retaining family or client fidelity. Loading, unavailable, capacity, waiting, expired, conflict and exhausted use the source's actual state wording. Capacity and waiting indicate waiting; expired and conflict describe the request; exhausted uses the source's unavailable wording. Image freshness never implies action availability. Passive preview descriptions are not a live announcement. A loading attempt capture is evidence of an attempt, not proof that a loading frame was painted.

Snap selection uses `aria-current="true"`: the target region fills with the action colour, its label and detail turn on-action, it carries a soft action-coloured glow, and the renderer's `Selected` detail names it. Under a hovering fine pointer a region that is not selected previews in the action colour at 42% with a 70% edge, and the selected region takes the action hover colour; a pressed region takes the action pressed colour. Keyboard focus uses the shared blue focus ring, independent of selection. Region selection does not apply a snap. Apply is enabled only when the shell is ready, no competing choice exists, the snap capability is present and the proposal exists. Its unavailable label is `Snapping unavailable`. An enabled Apply uses the affirmative fill with a soft action-coloured glow; an unavailable Apply keeps the essential border on the recessed fill with secondary text. Disabled controls retain semantics and readable text; a disabled region takes the recessed fill and secondary text without glow. Only the selected region and an enabled Apply carry a glow; high contrast and effects off remove both, and high contrast, effects off and reduced transparency paint the screen frames with the opaque recessed surface.

A picker card whose control carries `aria-current="true"` or `aria-selected="true"` takes the selected wash and a brand-text border at 55%; a hovered card shows a stronger hairline.

Focus and selected states change immediately. Enabled pointer fills follow shared motion tokens. Neither picker nor snap invokes the application portal. Escape, Close and keyboard traversal follow the existing popup policy. Selection and apply dispatch the renderer's scoped identities, publication and lease; a receipt does not establish placement.

## Geometry and renderer requirements

Before targets become interactive, the host measures the longest admitted label, title, state and detail at the committed scale and text spacing. If the normal fixed row cannot contain them, it publishes a taller stable row or a stacked text-first fixed-slot profile. If even that cannot contain the full content in the available host, it uses a text-first fixed-slot fallback with sufficient height and a scrollport. A change of profile invalidates the publication and lease before renewed input; a held press never transfers. Image arrival alone never causes this negotiation. These profiles require an explicit typed presentation projection and sizing negotiation; the current renderer does not emit one. The specimen measures before it admits its static controls; production admission remains a host requirement. D01 integration must implement and test this gate before enabling targets at those profiles.

The legacy preview lane retains lifecycle, paint and release handshakes, allowlisted handles, keyed nodes and hidden descriptions. No controlled-preview lane is implied. Source supplies only the actual visual variant, so the absence of a preview element must not collapse the reserved well in an admitted profile. Screen-reader text remains readable and distinct from the control's own label. Forced colours use system colours and preserve diagram shape, selection wording and focus: zones and Apply take ButtonText borders, the screen frames Canvas with a CanvasText border, the selected region Highlight with HighlightText, picker cards ButtonText borders with a Highlight border when selected, and the thumbnail pill Canvas with CanvasText; a focused control keeps colour forcing with a 2px Highlight outline. Reduced motion and all effects tiers retain static geometry.

## Profile admission contract

The source renderer does not emit `data-picker-profile`. The proposed host projection admits `expanded` or `stacked` only after it measures every visible caption with the active font, scale, width and text-spacing overrides. The host chooses one common `--wl-picker-row-height` that contains every state and content variant, including the image-absent variant. It invalidates the current publication and lease before changing profile or row height, waits for held input to end, then publishes the new geometry. The normal fixed profile is admitted only when every caption fits. A width or text change is a geometry change, while frame delivery and release are not. The stacked specimen illustrates this proposed projection; it does not claim the existing Elm renderer supplies it.

The host keeps targets unavailable until measurement and publication complete. If a 160 × 100 well leaves inadequate text space, the stacked profile places that well above the text. When the allocation cannot contain both, the host negotiates a text-first fixed-slot fallback with a scrollport. Full labels, details and fidelity remain visible. These admission and fallback rules require renderer and host work outside this package.

### Admission and lease sequence

Admission is a prerequisite, not a CSS breakpoint. D01 owns the integration and D06 owns this geometry contract. The current source has no measurement projection or gate, so picker controls must not be treated as admitted for unmeasured content. A compliant integration performs this sequence:

1. Render a noninteractive, offscreen measurement copy using the same width, fonts, scale, text-spacing, wrapping and control styles as the committed surface. Include every current title, action label and detail, all possible lifecycle and local-state labels, both fidelity strings, and the empty visual. Measure after fonts settle. Never announce or expose the measurement copy as an interactive control.
2. Admit the normal fixed row only if all content fits inside its bounds. Otherwise measure a common expanded row, then a stacked row. When the well and full text cannot fit together, negotiate a text-first fixed-slot row that retains full names, state and fidelity and uses a scrollport. The well remains reserved for the lifetime of that admitted profile. No label is clipped or hidden to make a profile fit.
3. Bind the profile and rounded-up common row height to the exact publication, lease, width, typography and content revision. Keep family targets unavailable until that geometry is committed and acknowledged. During a held input, retain the admitted rectangles; invalidate authority before a geometry change and never retarget the press. Re-measure, issue a new publication and lease, then enable input only after the new presentation is acknowledged.
4. A frame, icon, image removal or paint/release event changes only the content of the reserved well. It never changes row size or publication. A change to any measured text, font, available width, scale or spacing invalidates admission. The source-defined preview lifecycle and native release handshake continue independently.

The specimen demonstrates local measurement and admission for its static fixtures. It does not implement the production publication and lease protocol. Real-renderer verification covers 320px effective width, 200% scale and zoom, long titles, all state and fidelity labels, WCAG text spacing, frame delivery and removal while a pointer is held, and stale publication/lease rejection. These checks remain integration work until performed in the host.

## Measured slot projection

The host projects the common measured state, title, fidelity, action-label and action-detail slot heights through `--wl-picker-state-slot`, `--wl-picker-title-slot`, `--wl-picker-fidelity-slot`, `--wl-picker-label-slot` and `--wl-picker-detail-slot`, as well as `--wl-picker-row-height` for expanded, stacked and text-first profiles. Each value covers the greatest wrapped height in that slot across every family and every possible lifecycle visual for the admitted content revision. The normal and expanded profiles use these tracks alongside the 160 × 100px well. The stacked profile puts the well above the text. The text-first profile puts the full text first and retains a 64 × 40px visual slot after it. All profiles use one common measured row height. Every admitted profile uses these explicit tracks, including stacked and text-first. The static specimen measures its fixtures and lifecycle wording before admitting them; the source does not currently project these values.

Snap zones keep a 1px border in every state, so selection changes colour without moving the zone or its captions. The source's Selected detail is the non-colour cue. A focus ring is independent. Changes to snap column count are also geometry changes and use the host's publication and lease invalidation before renewed input.

## Specimen admission

The specimen loads the canonical bootstrap and the shared documentation stylesheet and starts picker surfaces inert. Each surface sits on a stage painted with its theme canvas; Night and Dawn pairs share one grid row and the same dimensions, while popup surfaces keep their surface, raised and recessed tokens. Its local measurement includes all displayed text and lifecycle wording, chooses a common profile and slot heights, and admits the static targets only after projection. A resize or font change retires the specimen targets before measurement; resize-observer callbacks only record the change and run retirement and measurement in the next animation frame, so attribute changes never resize an observed popup during observer delivery. Label measurement keeps the inline padding of the real label, including the reserved unavailable-cue slot. A held pointer defers re-admission until release. The fixed normal well is 160 × 100px even when the image is absent. The specimen does not implement a native lease, and its admission demonstration does not replace the renderer and host integration.
