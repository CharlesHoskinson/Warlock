# Window switcher

## Overview

The switcher is a Windows-style Alt-Tab row of window cards above a compact action row; one card at a time is the candidate. It chooses among observed window families without changing the desktop window focus or stacking while browsing. A committed activation is an intent; a subsequent native observation establishes the resulting focus. The preferred documentation width is 720px; the allocated host surface remains authoritative.

## Anatomy and hierarchy

The actual `.surface-popup[data-mode="switcher"]` contains `h1`, a static `p[role="status"][aria-live="off"]`, and `.surface-controls[role="group"]`. Its first child is `[role="listbox"][aria-label="Switch windows"]`; keyed buttons with `[role="option"]` and `data-surface-control` beginning `switcher:family:` follow the source entry order. Its second child is the separate `Window switcher actions` group. The controls retain source order: `control:close`, `control:reverse`, `control:forward`, `control:commit`. The source names are Cancel window switcher, Previous window, Next window and Activate selected window. Labels wrap, including at enlarged text sizes. Detail is the source's Selected, Open or Minimized. The specimen presents these actual strings.

The listbox lays its options out as a wrapping row of window cards with 10px gaps, in source order. Each card is a quarter of the row less the gaps, never narrower than 7.5rem, so four cards sit across a 700px popup and fewer fit as text grows; a short last row is centred, and every card keeps one width. A card is at least 11rem high (11.25rem with a coarse pointer), with 14px inline and top padding, 12px bottom padding, 12px corners, a 1px hairline (text at 9%), the 4% tile fill and a 1px top highlight. A 2.75rem glyph plate with 11px corners sits in its top corner, holding one generic 24px window glyph in brand text; the title sits at the foot of the card in Space Grotesk 16px at 600 with a 1.3 line height, and the source detail follows 3px below in 12px secondary text. The renderer supplies no window image or application identity, so every card shows the same window glyph. Titles and details wrap.

The action group follows 14px below the cards, under a 1px hairline with 14px of padding above the actions. The popup is an inline-size container. From 30rem the group is a three-column grid (`1fr auto auto`): Cancel at the start of the first row, Previous and Next at its end, and Activate alone on the second row at the inline end. Below 30rem the four actions stack in one column. The breakpoint is in rem, so larger text reaches the single column sooner. Source order and 8px gaps remain intact. Actions are at least 2.25rem high (2.75rem with a coarse pointer), with 10px corners, 13px labels at 600 and an 18px glyph. The scrollport retains 6px of focus paint and scroll padding. At short heights the popup itself scrolls without a sticky header. Popup bounds follow the host allocation; no preferred width leaves unused host pixels.

## States and attention

`[role="option"][aria-selected="true"]` lifts the candidate card: the selected wash (the action fill at 22%, 30% under a hovering pointer and 36% while pressed), a brand-text border at 55%, the raised shadow, and four 14px corner brackets in brand text, each 2px thick, inset 6px inside the rounded card. Its glyph plate fills with the action colour and the glyph turns on-action, and its detail turns brand text at 600. The source detail says Selected. A card that is not the candidate shows only a stronger hairline under a hovering pointer. Candidate remains visible when focus moves to an action. The shared 2px blue outer focus outline, 2px offset and opaque gap belong to the actually focused control and remain additive to the candidate's depth. Cancel remains quiet in secondary text, Previous and Next have a text-at-20% outline on the tile fill, and Activate alone uses the affirmative fill with a soft action-coloured glow when enabled. The source-disabled button keeps disabled semantics, full opacity and secondary text. Unavailable Activate keeps a neutral essential boundary on the recessed fill together with the shared unavailable cue, so it still reads as a button. Unavailable actions never gain hover or press feedback. High contrast and effects off remove the card and Activate shadows; high contrast draws every card with the essential border, the candidate with a brand-text border, and Previous and Next with the essential border, and preserves the candidate wording. Forced colours draw cards with CanvasText borders, the candidate with a Highlight border and corner-clipped Highlight brackets on a noninteractive overlay, the glyph plate as Canvas with a CanvasText ring, and action buttons with ButtonText borders; a focused card keeps colour forcing with a 2px Highlight outline.

The active application uses its separate red desktop contour and activity marker. The observed focused window uses its neutral guarded edge, beacon and taskbar marker. Neither derives from the switcher candidate. Missing typed presentation facts produce neutral desktop treatment. The switcher displays no timestamps or inferred application activity, and no window thumbnails until the renderer supplies them.

## Behaviour and keyboard

Opening, cycling, cancellation and exit are immediate. Native Alt+Tab and Shift+Alt+Tab cycle when delivered; release commits when the native chord is delivered. Local controls expose Previous, Next, Activate and Cancel. The source status describes Tab or Right, Shift+Tab or Left, Enter and Escape. Escape cancels. A pointer or local choice changes the candidate, not the native window. Commit uses the captured identity and authority. A closed or stale publication cannot commit. The frozen entry set excludes later arrivals; retirement reconciles surviving entries. Entry and exit never use a portal or fade. Focus return follows the controller's observed focus policy.

## Accessibility and renderer requirements

Preserve keyed option nodes, `aria-selected`, listbox and option roles, action grouping and `data-surface-control` identities. Do not infer keyboard focus from selection or duplicate the option's accessible name in CSS. Focus paint must stay visible at every scroll edge. Retain source names and activation semantics. The host must provide actual window focus and activity facts to the separate desktop presentation. Window thumbnails for the cards are renderer data: the card needs a host-provided, decorative window image for each family, keyed to the family identity and bound to the same publication and lease as the option, with the generic glyph as the fallback. Per-application icons for the glyph plate need the same keyed, decorative projection. Neither may change the option's accessible name, identity or order. An unavailable/slashed cue for a disabled action is a shared renderer requirement; CSS alone cannot supply a semantic unavailable indication. At 320px and 200% text, cards wrap to fewer per row, actions stack, and controls scroll rather than shrink or clip.
