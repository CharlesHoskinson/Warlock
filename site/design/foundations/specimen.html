Window focus, application activity, navigation, keyboard delivery and control focus describe distinct facts. Try the desktop.

Cues and keyboard focus

Each theme places the focused-window, active-application and candidate cues side by side, once with effects on and once with effects off. Effects off removes soft shadows; the focused edge and title beacon, the inset activity contour and the candidate brackets keep their geometry and colour. Each theme also has an enabled affirmative action and a secondary action. Press Tab to move keyboard focus through them; the readout names the control that holds focus.
