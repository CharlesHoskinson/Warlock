# Foundations

## Overview

Warlock uses Lacquer: mineral-black surfaces, warm ivory text and restrained red identity. The taskbar and every popup are frosted glass over the wallpaper, arranged as a Windows-style desktop: a taskbar across the top, a Start-style launcher, a card window switcher, Task View columns, a snap layouts flyout, notification cards and a Settings page of switches, segments and swatches. Dawn applies the same hierarchy to warm paper. High contrast uses black surfaces and white boundaries, and its glass is opaque. [Color](color.html), [Effects](effects.html) and the [attention specimen](specimen.html) show the system. [Try the desktop](../../test-drive/index.html).

## Typography and spacing

Inter supplies body, labels and detail. Space Grotesk supplies titles and card display lines. JetBrains Mono Regular supplies paths and shortcuts. All fonts load locally with `font-display: swap` and synthesis disabled. The window title is 20/28px at 700; section title 16/24 at 600; body 14/20 at 400; label 14/20 at 500; detail 12/16 at 400. In the shell skin a popup title is Space Grotesk 22px at 600 with a 1.25 line height and -0.01em tracking, and the popup status is 13px secondary text at a 1.45 line height. Switcher window cards set their title in Space Grotesk 16px at 600 and Task View workspace headers in Space Grotesk 15px at 600. The root applies the committed 100, 125, 150 or 200% text scale once. Padding remains in CSS pixels.

The spacing scale is 0, 2, 4, 6, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24, 32, 40, 48 and 64px. Label and detail are 2px apart. In list arrangements related groups are 16px apart and major groups 24px apart; the shell skin's grids and cards set their own group spacing, stated in each area specification. Controls reserve a 6px focus gutter. Standard controls are at least 36px high, two-line rows 56px and fields 44px. Coarse pointers use 44px, 60px and 44px respectively. Text wraps without shrinking.

## Anatomy and selectors

`SurfaceRenderer.elm` renders `.surface-popup[data-mode]` with a heading, a static `aria-live="off"` status and `.surface-controls`. Controls carry `data-surface-control`; fields carry `data-surface-field`. Labels and details use `.control-label` and `.control-detail`. A bounded flex column places scrolling in `.surface-controls`, with a 6px gutter. The shared control sheet gives the popup a 16px block and 10px inline inset; the shell skin sets 20px at the top and sides and 16px at the bottom, and Applications uses 16px at the top. Each area stylesheet arranges `.surface-controls` as a list, grid or row flow in source order. At short heights the whole popup scrolls. Renderer geometry remains authoritative.

The shared action treatments are affirmative filled, secondary outlined, subtle utility and consequential outlined. One enabled affirmative action appears per independent task block. Destructive confirmation uses danger text on neutral chrome, with a danger outline in the list arrangement and a danger edge and tint on the shell confirmation card; Cancel precedes Confirm. A disabled control retains its native disabled semantics, secondary text and full opacity. A focus-only unavailable control retains `data-focus-only` and `aria-disabled`; it remains reachable but does not activate. A decorative slash marks unavailable controls; native disabled or aria-disabled semantics remain authoritative. The slashed ring paints as the label's own background in a reserved inline-end slot that every popup label keeps in every state, aligned with the first line. Marking a control unavailable therefore never changes its dimensions, and the cue never shares a pseudo-element with selection bars, checks or diagrams.

## Shell skin and glyphs

`design/foundations/shell-skin.css` is the shared Lacquer shell skin. It loads last in the test-drive taskbar and popup documents (`test-drive/runtime/bar.html` and `popup.html`), after the shared control sheet and every area stylesheet. Popups are one glass panel: the surface colour at 78% opacity, the 1px essential border, a 1px inner hairline of text at 9%, a 1px top highlight and 14px corners. The taskbar is a vertical gradient from the raised surface at 74% to the canvas at 70%, with an inner bottom hairline and a 1px shadow line. Translucent fills come from text colour: tiles at 4%, hover at 7% and press at 11%. The selected wash is the action fill at 22%, and 30% under a hovering pointer. High contrast, effects off and reduced transparency replace the glass with the opaque surface and raised colours. Forced colours paint the bar and popups Canvas with CanvasText and remove the hairline shadows.

`assets/shared/glyphs.css` defines 53 `--wl-glyph-*` tokens, one for each original 24px line glyph in `assets/shared/glyphs/` (1.8px stroke, round caps and joins). Two further images draw the magnifier inside the Applications search field in Night and Dawn. The skin paints a glyph as a mask on `.control-label::before`, filled with `currentColor`, so it follows text colour, theme, secondary text on unavailable controls, high contrast and forced colours (CanvasText, or GrayText on unavailable Files, System, menu and jump controls). The default glyph slot is 1.25rem square with a 0.625rem gap before the label. Glyphs mark fixed shell controls only: taskbar utilities, Close, Refresh, the launcher's pin, jump and launch actions, switcher actions, Task View filters and actions, snap Apply, Notifications policy tiles, Settings help, switches, theme and text-size choices and saves, Files destinations and System actions. Every control keeps its text name and its accessible name; a glyph is decoration and never replaces a label. Application groups, launch results, switcher cards and Task View cards carry no per-application identity: launch results share one glyph, and switcher and Task View cards share one generic window glyph.

Standalone specimens load neither the skin nor the glyph sheet. They show each area stylesheet's own arrangement on opaque surfaces, and glyph slots there are empty space. The Files and System menu arrangements are scoped to the runtime popup document, so their specimens show the list arrangement.

The test drive draws its simulated desktop in `test-drive/desktop.css`: original Night and Dawn wallpapers from `test-drive/stage/assets`, the taskbar frame blurred at 26px and the popup frame at 30px with 14px corners and a large soft cast shadow, and client windows with a 1px edge, 10px corners, a 2rem titlebar and a soft cast shadow that deepens on the focused window. Client areas are 90% opaque (86% when unfocused) and carry no backdrop blur, so the wallpaper's warmth shows faintly; on the native desktop Hyprland draws window blur. Its focused-title beacon is 3px wide, 45% of the titlebar height and rounded. Effects off and high contrast remove every blur and make titlebars and client areas opaque. This presentation belongs to the simulated desktop; native window decoration follows the attention rules below.

## Attention and state

A focused window uses a neutral 2px edge, inner and outer 1px opaque guards, raised titlebar and a 3 × 14px title beacon. An unfocused window uses the unfocused edge, surface titlebar and secondary title text. Activity uses a separate inset brand contour and Active wording; on the taskbar it is the selected wash and a 28px brand pill. Candidate uses four corner brackets, a selected wash and candidate wording: 12px neutral brackets on menu rows and 14px brand brackets on switcher cards. Selection uses a brand cue together with the existing selected wording: a 3px brand bar and check on Task View filters, a brand ring and check badge on Settings theme cards, a short brand pill on segmented choices, a filled radio on shortcut rows, and the action fill on a snap region. Confirmed keyboard recipient uses focus-colored paired chevrons or a recipient label. Control focus uses a 2px focus outline, 2px offset and opaque 2px gap. These facts remain independent.

The renderer projects switcher candidates with `[role="option"][aria-selected="true"]`, menu checks with `[role="menuitemcheckbox"][aria-checked="true"]`, Task View filters with `aria-pressed`, and Settings and snap choices with their mode-specific `aria-current`. Settings and notification toggles use their existing `aria-pressed`; Help uses `aria-expanded`. No global `aria-current` rule assigns meaning. Host observations supply window focus, activity and recipient independently. Missing facts remain neutral.

## Depth and motion

Fields, rows and tiled clients are flush. Floating clients and taskbar use rest elevation, detached popups use raised elevation and separate blocking dialogs use dialog elevation. In the shell skin the taskbar elevation is its 1px shadow line and the popup frame casts the popup's shadow. Inside a popup, cards that stand apart from the list (the selected switcher card, Task View window cards and live notification cards) carry small decorative shadows, and the primary action of a task (Activate, Apply, an enabled Save) carries a soft action-coloured glow. Shadows remain decorative. Focus changes do not change elevation. High contrast and effects-off remove soft shadows, highlights and glow while retaining guards and outlines.

Shell surfaces enter and leave immediately. Selection, focus, disabled state and outcome changes are immediate. Enabled pointer feedback on a quiet control uses 90ms for hover and for release back to hover and 60ms for press, with `cubic-bezier(.2,0,0,1)`; leaving hover settles at once. The transition exists only while a fine pointer hovers or presses a quiet control: one that is not a candidate, selected, pressed, checked, expanded, busy, focus-only or unavailable. A control that becomes a candidate or selected, pressed, checked, unavailable or busy changes at once, including under the pointer; the one residual case, a selection or candidate leaving a hovered control, settles over the 90ms pointer timing. Motion preference projected by Elm takes precedence; unknown motion starts suppressed. Reduced motion removes the portal and makes durations zero.

The application portal begins only after an observation establishes a newly mapped window or removal of the same incarnation. It never follows launch intent, Submitted, Unknown, a focus change or a shell popup. Opening lasts 520ms: ignite 0–120, expand 120–380, resolve 380–460 and settle 460–520. Closing lasts 360ms: release 0–90, contract 90–270 and extinguish 270–360. The decorative plane never clips, moves or delays live input. Reduced effects use a decorative 120ms opening or 90ms closing fade; off is immediate. One portal runs at a time, with at most one full start in a rolling second. Cancellation and visibility loss settle immediately.

## Accessibility and content

Keyboard focus remains visible at each scroll edge. Forced colors use system colors, actual boundaries and outlines; the glass becomes an opaque Canvas surface. Reduced transparency replaces the glass with opaque surfaces. Static status text stays quiet; the existing announcement owner communicates changes. Pending uses a clock and waiting words, unknown a question diamond and unconfirmed wording, refusal a stop glyph and reason. Untyped text remains neutral and never acquires an inferred outcome. Unknown never retries a mutation automatically.

## Renderer requirements

CSS reads state and never creates authority. Typed projections are required for observed window focus, application activity, confirmed recipient, typed outcome and visible unavailable cues. Glyph identity comes only from fixed `data-surface-control` identities. Per-application icons, window thumbnails, workspace and notification wrappers, Settings group headings, window-menu command identities and typed confirmation consequences are renderer data that CSS cannot supply; each area specification states its requirement. Keyed identities, composition events, focus return, accessible names, publication and lease stay with Elm. The host validates effect cycle and target completion, handles cancellation and a bounded watchdog, and retires input immediately when removal is observed. The specimen illustrates independent facts; it is not native evidence.
