# Exact renderer terminal witnesses

Fresh source derivative of reviewed frozen RecoveryV15-v2; no native acceptance.

A recorded renderer PID/start can still name an unreaped zombie. An exact pidfd that reports POLLIN proves that selected process lifetime has exited; the PID need not disappear before durable cancellation refresh or resumed retired-resource validation. Missing or changed PID/start also proves the old lifetime absent, and grants no signaling or write authority over a replacement. Positive typed identities remain mandatory. Live same PID/start with an unreadable/nonterminal pidfd refuses cancellation. Unexpected procfs/pidfd errors quarantine.

The observation opens a pidfd for the recorded PID, rechecks the recorded start, and queries that descriptor. It is read-only and never signals. A terminal exact lifetime cannot resume execution. A nonterminal observation may conservatively refuse if the process exits immediately afterward.
