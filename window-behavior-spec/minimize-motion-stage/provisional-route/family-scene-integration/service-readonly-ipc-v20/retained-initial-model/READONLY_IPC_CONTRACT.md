## Preservation and restart

Read-only connection closure grants no native effect result, actor cancellation,
endpoint settlement or renderer output authority. It cannot waive any inherited
Keeper group, native effect/export uncertainty, renderer or source disposal gate.
The controller still rechecks exact current receipt/member geometry and complete
family before promotion and native writes. Late data after supersession cannot
acquire authority through this adapter. The controller2 second deadline and
existing call timeouts are unchanged.

Restart must parse retained bounded connection history and its source, issuer,
request/phase/FD ownership. An unfinished old connection is read-only work, not a
successful observation: it remains unusable and cannot be replayed as an effect.
Old service lifetime/whole Keeper/job/resource closure and fresh current startup
observations remain mandatory. Live per-actor cancellation must later bind these
read-only jobs to its own drain and freshness proof; it remains unimplemented.
