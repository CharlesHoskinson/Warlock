Arming and the signal callback do not run a collection loop or wait for the worker.

The worker receives immutable value copies and owns local readonly descriptors, byte arrays and RAII resources. It cannot access Registry, Provider, QObject, QPointer, QQmlEngine, QML, getters, source/current/receipt callbacks, GUI state or helper controls. It may collect at most 16 complete candidate epochs within the original absolute two-second cap from Started; budget checks before and after each operation prevent acceptance of late data. No sleeps or observation barriers. Blocking filesystem I/O cannot be interrupted safely: a late operation must fail, and its still-running job stays in the terminal inventory until normal completion. Failure does not justify detaching or killing the worker or helper.
