## Why

Warlock uses a coherent black and red design language across its surfaces. The language distinguishes brand, information, focus and outcomes while retaining observation and native qualification boundaries.

## What Changes

Revision 2 specifies Lacquer tokens, contrast, interaction states, independent attention cues, elevation, the observed-window portal and the Elm and CSS presentation contract. It revises the affected design and layered-window requirements by identity and adds explicit requirements for the complete system.

## Capabilities

### Modified Capabilities

- `warlock-design-language`: Lacquer identity, states, tokens, accessibility, motion and presentation.
- `warlock-layered-window-language`: independent attention, neutral window focus, depth and bounded effects.

## Impact

The token compiler, Elm presentation, CSS, compositor appearance adapter, documentation and qualification fixtures require correspondence to these contracts. Original requirements, scenarios, deadlines, evidence and native gates remain binding. Browser evidence does not establish native behavior.
