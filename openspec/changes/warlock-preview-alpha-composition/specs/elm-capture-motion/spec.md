## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Independently verified composition environment
The system SHALL bind a controlled preview composition claim to independently observed native output conditions.

EARS WRLK-ALPHA-001: WHEN comparing captured family alpha against a controlled backdrop, qualification SHALL prove that actual native backdrop in the independent output, retain every crop pixel and restore private fixture configuration before original final GUI cases.

#### Scenario: Actual black versus assumed default
- **WHEN** a fixture assumes black but native output remains#111111
- **THEN** acceptance is refused and failed evidence retained; configuring black and independently observing it precedes the original scoped capture in the corrected derivative

### Requirement: Whole crop composition and physical ownership
The system SHALL qualify borders and nonopaque margins alongside body pixels under original native context and ownership.

EARS WRLK-ALPHA-002: WHEN admitting a whole-crop alpha claim, all RGB/native output alpha comparisons SHALL pass across the original crop under its original native-issued scope/deadline, and physical mapping/FD/export/producer retirement SHALL precede terminal qualification.

#### Scenario: Identity and tint include nonopaque pixels
- **WHEN** actual identity/tint programs produce a family PNG
- **THEN** opaque, partial and transparent pixels are included in the independent source-over comparison, with no window relocation or job renewal

### Requirement: Measurement facts distinct from acceptance
The system SHALL distinguish completed pixel measurement from accepted native fidelity.

EARS WRLK-ALPHA-003: WHEN a valid measurement reports disagreement, it SHALL retain passed:false as facts and the final native verifier SHALL refuse acceptance after original assertions/normal cleanup; measurement completion SHALL NOT grant pixel authority.

#### Scenario: Disagreement reaches terminal oracle
- **GIVEN** decoded facts disagree with independent native pixels
- **WHEN** original assertions and owned teardown complete
- **THEN** the mandatory terminal fidelity assertion fails without weakening its predicate or original process/deadline requirements

### Requirement: Complete alpha and backdrop remain mandatory
The system SHALL keep controlled composition separate from full preview and coherent release acceptance.

EARS WRLK-ALPHA-004: WHEN publishing a controlled black-backdrop result, the verifier SHALL retain partially transparent body, arbitrary backdrop/blur dependency/privacy, general shader/color/HDR/transform/output/hardware and measured GPU/driver obligations until independently qualified on the coherent release tuple.
