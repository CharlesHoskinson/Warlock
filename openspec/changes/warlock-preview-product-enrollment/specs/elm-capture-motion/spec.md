# ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: WARLOCK-ENROLL-001 — Own native catalog

WHEN actual window inventory is read, the provider SHALL validate its own binding and exact positive request/sequence/revision, at most256 unique positive incarnation rows, bounded UTF8 labels and exact minimized Booleans before typed admission; catalog metadata SHALL NOT grant capture authority or fabricate native scopes, clocks, jobs or terminal outcomes.

#### Scenario: WARLOCK-ENROLL-001 strict catalog domain

- GIVEN a current own snapshot including minimized windows and counters above JavaScript safe integer range
- WHEN the inventory is decoded
- THEN every exact own record is retained losslessly and foreign, malformed, duplicated, overbound or wrongly typed data are rejected without changing retained ownership

### Requirement: WARLOCK-ENROLL-002 — Actual picker demand and native authority

WHEN ordinary admitted picker controls request previews, enrollment SHALL correlate their exact native incarnation identities with current own catalog facts, obtain native scope authority under the provider own grant, and use the same immutable Elm policy; presentation demand SHALL NOT authorize foreign sources or fabricate metadata-only NativeScope.

#### Scenario: WARLOCK-ENROLL-002 automatic ordinary entries

- GIVEN actual native windows and ordinary product picker controls without fixed QA subjects
- WHEN the picker opens or its visible entries change
- THEN authorized correlated entries enroll through the own native scope, and absent/unknown scope remains distinct from denial without fabricated capture or clock

### Requirement: WARLOCK-ENROLL-003 — Shared bounded ownership and removed entries

WHEN enrolled entries disappear, change domain, close or yield allocator capacity, the provider SHALL retain original jobs, readers, clocks/deadlines, native retirement and exact ACK identity until physical completion, bound live capture allocations by the existing two-item/shared byte/readers policy, and SHALL NOT renew old deadlines, evict live resources or replay an unknown result to enable a new entry.

#### Scenario: WARLOCK-ENROLL-003 multiple entries and late retirement

- GIVEN multiple ordinary entries and an old entry with pending native retirement or held readers
- WHEN another entry requests capacity or the picker closes
- THEN shared bounds hold, new admission waits safely, original cleanup settles once, and slot reuse follows exact physical retirement and terminal ACK
