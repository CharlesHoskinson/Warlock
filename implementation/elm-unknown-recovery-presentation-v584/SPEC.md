Keep the reason discoverable when the disabled mutation is skipped by keyboard traversal. Provide a separately named, enabled observation/reconciliation control with an accessible description; keep unrelated valid controls available. Observation refresh updates knowledge only and cannot certify settlement or clear an unresolved reservation.
