# Sampled client-tree revision contract

Additive to frozen242/417, right-click24/48 and original preview13.

- TREE-01: WHEN any sampled root or child surface commits, attaches, changes
  viewport/offset/order, detaches or retires, the native producer SHALL advance
  or invalidate the enrolled client-tree content context before new publication.
- TREE-02: WHEN capture begins and completes, the native producer SHALL validate
  every sampled surface membership/incarnation and one coherent content revision
  under the original deadline without interpreting web metadata as authority.
- TREE-03: IF a tree observer cannot cover every sampled surface or exhausts its
  native identity domain, the producer SHALL refuse production eligibility and
  preserve existing ownership and physical retirement obligations.
- TREE-04: WHEN a surface or producer actor retires, native listeners SHALL drain
  without retargeting a reused resource, renewing deadlines or declaring pixel
  destruction from a closed UI/socket alone.

Use a fresh source derivative, selected coupled Quint traces and actual native
child-only commit/reparent/destruction cases. Family/modal/popup composition and
provider-v6/WebKit integration remain separate unmet release obligations.
