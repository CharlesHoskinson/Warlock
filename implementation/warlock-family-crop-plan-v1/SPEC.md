# Native family full crop planner

The current accepted native family plane is monitor-sized and clips contributors outside that canvas. Complete family fidelity needs a framebuffer covering the union of all actual contributor extents, with a recorded native origin and outward pixel rounding. This planner computes that union without changing desktop geometry or accepting a frontend membership list. Extents must come from owning native window decoration/popup bounds and applied surface projections, under the exact family context; wiring those reads, renderer projection, versioned crop FD and typed Elm consumption remains required.

Empty, malformed/nonfinite/nonpositive, over-capacity, unrepresentable or over4096-pixel image extents refuse. Capacity512 covers at most256 validated native family members plus256 sampled applied surface rows; image storage uses the original PNG plan and reservation accounting. Finite positive scales are computed with floor/ceil outward rounding, including negative origins. CPU/model projection is not native fractional-output, crop/fidelity or production preview13 qualification. Current production eligibility remains false and all original GUI gates/deadlines remain unchanged.
