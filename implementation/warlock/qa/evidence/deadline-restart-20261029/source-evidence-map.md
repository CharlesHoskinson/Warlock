**Original scenario is not independently established by the retained receipts inspected.** ELM-ARC-013 requires each original acceptance deadline retained through retries, queueing, reconciliation and frontend restart. `architecture-013`: request nearing original deadline; renderer restarts; deadline remains unchanged. Verification explicitly allows a preserved native **or bridge** trace, with matching scenario receipts and artifact hashes. The original does not require a GUI, physical native-clock reading, all hardware or an unrelated Main route for this witness. No product defect is asserted from an absent witness.
