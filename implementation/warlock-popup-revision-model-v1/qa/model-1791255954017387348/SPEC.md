# Popup revision projection and actual TreeRevision replay

This additive proof supports POPUP-01–04 in popup-source-revisions-v1 and W03/W09. It retains every original S09 scenario and deadline. It does not grant capture, production family fidelity or hardware acceptance.

WHEN the native membership/applied commit/geometry projection changes, TreeRevision SHALL advance a monotonic epoch; repeated identical observations SHALL retain it. WHEN popup membership retires and returns, its replacement SHALL have a fresh identity. IF graph identity cannot be proved, the projection SHALL be unavailable until a complete subsequent observation. These five explicitly selected Quint traces are replayed through the actual unchanged TreeRevision C++ header. The four valid traces also require separate native root/popup observer replay; synthetic identifiers do not prove actual allocator-address reuse.
