# Qt journal consumer boundary

The consumer binds complete raw QWindow pointer/key intervals to an explicit
role, instance, mapping generation and actual Wayland resource. It preserves
untracked recipients and other roles when checking the complete interval.
QWidget propagation is reported separately. Mouse source and press/release
modifier states are explicit, with Qt event/source constants compiled from the
installed owning Qt6 headers. The parser closes the held250 event vocabulary.

The protected tests use synthetic journal rows and actual compiled enum values.
They do not launch Qt applications or prove native input, focus, modality,
presentation, popup grabs, geometry or retirement. globalQt positions describe
Qt client coordinates and do not independently establish compositor positions.
The future native runner must separately correlate those positions and resource
identities with native and raw Wayland evidence within the original deadlines.

The first failed profile-domain test is retained alongside later reports.
Source and dependency inventories are frozen only after final source review.
