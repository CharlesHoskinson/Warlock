# Complete diagnostic collection acceptance

There are exactly eight independent complete-image gates: raw and composed RGBA
for two outputs at two held progress values. Any failed pixel comparison stays
failed while independent remaining observations continue. Partial collection
cannot pass. Duplicate slots cannot replace earlier evidence. Source, identity,
presentation binding, protocol or native-authority failure closes the campaign
immediately and cannot be recovered by later pixels. Completed acceptance
requires all eight distinct slots, no failed pixels and no fatal violation.

This model checks result bookkeeping and refusal. It does not establish actual
GPU output, native presentation, cadence, taskbar actions or physical behavior.
Actual V6 reports retain four raw and four composed complete-image comparisons;
their failures remain authoritative. Prior frozen packets are unchanged.
