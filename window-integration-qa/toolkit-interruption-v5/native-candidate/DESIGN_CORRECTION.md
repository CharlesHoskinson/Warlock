# Canonical atlas coordinate correction contract

Version15 and the failing native artifacts remain unchanged. The root-run matrix at ~/.cache/window-atlas-v15-nested/snapshot-smoke.json proved correct metadata and incorrect pixels: client data.damage was intersected with its old output-space windowBox before scissor, and rectangle rounding TOP_LEFT used the unmodified output-space box. Rotated outputs also inverse-transform rounding coordinates against monitor geometry even with RPT_EXPORT. A projected translation alone cannot establish a complete atlas.

Each render element must enter the canonical atlas domain exactly once BEFORE surface damage/visible-region clipping or quad rounding are computed. Surface positions are logical global coordinates and normalize by pixelOffset/outputScale; Rect/Tex/Border boxes and clips are output pixels and normalize by pixelOffset. Surface-generated internal Tex elements are already canonical and must not normalize twice. The atlas has no output transform; monitor-transformed shader rounding boxes must be inverted into the corresponding canonical rectangle before public CShader uniform setters update their cache. Bar scissor and direct shadow boxes must share that domain. Native client/monitor geometry is never mutated.

The proposed implementation is a capture-only exact-ABI adapter around public IElementRenderer::drawElement plus public shader float2 setters. It uses an active atlas context outside regular render, type dispatch for supported elements, recursive scope tracking, and per-shader pending bounding-box uniforms. Every capture must fail explicitly for an unsupported render path or unmatched shader coordinate pair. Hooks are idle outside atlas rendering, require unique exact ELF symbols after the plugin ABI guard, and must unload cleanly. The adapter is implemented and compiled offline; build-report.json records exact hashes. No version16 native load or pixel acceptance has occurred. Blur/mirror/motion-blur/transformed-window/unrecognized custom elements explicitly fail until supported. No feature is silently dropped from successful atlas captures.

Normal rendering and private CShader.uniformStatus equivalence remain materially bounded as in version15: public setters maintain coherence but do not restore all previous private uniform contents. Subsequent normal output-frame pixel equality is mandatory. Native all-transform and full gap/shadow/caption/client pixel replay remains the acceptance gate, with no weakened clipping or pixel thresholds.
